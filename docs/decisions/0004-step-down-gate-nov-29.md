# ADR-0004 — Step-down decision is dated: Nov 29, 2026 (EMS E3 recon mid-November)
Date: 2026-11-02 · Status: Accepted

## Context
The bridge seat (ADR-0001) renews Nov 29, 2026. EMS E3 (~$22.10/mo) is the candidate step-down: the MD-102 lab (Intune/Autopilot) survives on EMS E3, while MS-102 preparation needs Business Premium workload services. Placement runs Nov 9–29, so the licence question and the placement block end together.

## Decision
Decide at the Nov 29 gate, informed by a mid-November EMS E3 recon (which Premium workloads the December–January plan actually still needs). No change before the gate; no silent renewal past it.

## Alternatives rejected
- Step down immediately on Nov 2 — discards Premium workload coverage before the front-load week that uses it
- Let the bridge renew indefinitely by default — a subscription drifting past its decision date is exactly the billing pattern this lab exists to unlearn

## Consequences
One calendar item owns this decision: **2026-11-29 — bridge renewal / EMS E3 step-down gate.** The weekly status file carries it as the Cost line until it closes.
